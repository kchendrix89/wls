FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about White Label Suite.

What is a credit and how are they used? ▾
A credit is used to conduct a search or enrichment. Each search type costs a specific amount: People Search costs 3 credits per record (with contact data), Local Search costs 1 credit per company (only if an email is returned), Company and Domain Search cost 1 credit per result, and Influencer Search costs 2–5 credits per record. If a search doesn't return at least one email, no credits are charged. AI features are completely free and never consume credits.
Can I really white-label this platform with my own branding? ▾
Yes — with the White-Label & Resell plan, you get full branding control. You can add your agency's logo, set your own domain, customize colors, and your clients will never see any reference to White Label Suite. You can also create and manage unlimited client accounts and set your own pricing for credits.
How accurate is the contact data? ▾
All data is sourced in real-time — we never sell old or recycled database records. Every email address and phone number is verified at the time of the search, which means you get significantly higher deliverability and connect rates compared to static lead databases. We only charge a credit when we actually find a verified email for a record.
Do unused credits roll over each month? ▾
Yes — on all premium plans (Startup, Growth, Enterprise, and White-Label & Resell), unused search credits roll over to the following month. You never lose credits you've paid for. On the White-Label & Resell annual plan, your 60,000 credits are available across the full year.
What integrations are available? ▾
White Label Suite natively integrates with HighLevel, Keap, Groove Funnels, and Sales Connector. It also connects with Zapier (giving you access to 5,000+ apps) and includes a built-in webhook so you can connect to any platform that accepts webhooks. Google API and OpenAI API integrations are also built in for enrichment and AI features.
Is there a money-back guarantee? ▾
Absolutely. All plans come with a 30-day money-back guarantee. If you're not completely satisfied within 30 days of purchasing, contact our support team and we'll issue a full refund — no questions asked.
